Who We Are

More Than
a Racing Team

Veloce Dynamics is a student-led STEM racing team that designs, builds, and races fully engineered cars. We compete at national level while developing the engineers and scientists of tomorrow.

Our multidisciplinary team spans aerodynamics, mechanical engineering, electronics, data science, and software — united by one goal: go faster, smarter.
